Privacy Policy

Bonne Nouvelle app (Android) · Last updated 30 October 2026

In short: Bonne Nouvelle needs no account and collects no personal data on its own servers. Your tasks, garden, favourites and settings stay on your phone. The only data that leaves it is what advertising (Google AdMob) and, if you remove ads, the purchase (Google Play and RevenueCat) require.

1. Who we are

Bonne Nouvelle is an app published by an independent developer. Every day it offers a piece of positive content (a quote, a fact, a mini-challenge, a news story), a to-do list and a garden that blooms over time.

2. What stays on your phone

The following data is stored only on your device. We have no access to it and it is never sent anywhere:

  • your settings (interests, reminder time, language, theme, reading speed);
  • your daily tasks, garden, streak, badges and favourites;
  • the list of content already shown (to avoid repeats).

Notifications are scheduled locally on the phone. Read aloud uses your device's text-to-speech engine. Share images are created on the phone and only sent to the app you choose. The widget reads the same local data.

Uninstalling the app erases all of this data. You can also reset your garden in Settings.

3. What the app downloads

To bring you new content, the app downloads public files without sending any information about you. As with any Internet connection, these sites can see your phone's IP address:

  • the app's online content, hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub's policy);
  • positive news feeds from Good News Network, Positive News and Reasons to be Cheerful;
  • the translation of a news headline into your language, through the MyMemory service: only the article's headline is sent.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure them and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and technical information (model, operating system, language);
  • your IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
  • interactions with ads (views, clicks) and diagnostic data.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent on first launch. You can change your mind at any time in Settings › Privacy choices. Without consent, Google only shows non-personalised or limited ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Google › Ads).

Learn more: how Google uses information from partner apps and Google's privacy policy.

5. "Remove ads" purchase

Payment is handled entirely by Google Play: we never see your payment details. To verify the purchase and let you restore it, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier generated by the app and the related purchase history (product, date, country, price). See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

7. Children

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not designed for children under 13.

8. Your rights

Since we keep no data about you, there is nothing for us to send, correct or delete. For data processed by Google or RevenueCat, you can exercise your rights (access, deletion, objection) directly with them through the links above. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date.

10. Contact

For any question, write to the developer contact address shown on Bonne Nouvelle's Google Play listing.
